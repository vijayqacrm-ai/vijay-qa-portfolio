QA APPROACH

Business process first. Risk always.

01

Understand the business flow

Start with the transaction and expected business outcome, not just individual screens.

02

Map the ecosystem

Identify CRM, ERP, APIs, middleware, data stores, third parties and asynchronous dependencies.

03

Prioritize risk

Focus deeper testing on business-critical flows, complex integrations, data risk and production impact.

04

Validate beyond UI

Where appropriate, validate API payloads, mappings, transformations, backend data and downstream results.

05

Drive defects to root cause

Use triage, evidence and cross-team collaboration to distinguish symptoms from underlying issues.

06

Make release risk visible

Combine test coverage, defects, environment readiness, dependencies and residual risk into release decisions.
