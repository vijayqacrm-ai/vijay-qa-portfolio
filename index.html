HOW I LEAD QA

Systematic QA leadership aligned to business risk.

01

Understand the business objective

Start with the transaction and expected business outcome, not just individual screens.

02

Map the ecosystem and dependencies

Identify CRM, ERP, APIs, middleware, data stores, third parties and asynchronous dependencies.

03

Prioritize risk

Focus deeper testing on business-critical flows, complex integrations, data risk and production impact.

04

Establish test strategy and ownership

Define what needs testing, how, and who owns each layer. Make expectations clear.

05

Validate with evidence

Use test cases, API validation, SQL checks, backend verification and logs to build credible evidence of readiness.

06

Make release risk visible

Combine test coverage, defects, environment readiness, dependencies and residual risk into clear release decisions.

Good QA is not simply executing more test cases. It is understanding where failure matters, creating the right evidence, making risk visible, and helping the team make informed release decisions.
